# About Me

I am a PhD researcher at the [Indian Institute of Science (IISc)](https://www.iisc.ac.in/), Bangalore, pursuing doctoral studies in Computational and Data Sciences. My research focuses on developing robust numerical solvers for simulating reacting flows with applications to combustion systems. I am passionate about computational methods that bridge theory, simulation, and practical engineering challenges.

## Research Interests

- **Computational Combustion:** Simulation of turbulent combustion, flame dynamics, and reactive flow phenomena
- **Numerical Methods:** Development and implementation of high-order accurate schemes for hyperbolic conservation laws
- **Reacting Flow Solvers:** Building robust, efficient solvers for complex chemical kinetics and fluid dynamics coupling
- **Uncertainty Quantification:** Characterizing model and parameter uncertainties in simulation-based engineering
- **High-Performance Computing:** Scalable algorithms for large-scale computational problems
